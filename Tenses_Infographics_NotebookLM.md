# ENGLISH TENSES INFOGRAPHICS

## 1. PRESENT SIMPLE

### Usages & Rules
- **Always True / General Truths**
  - *Example:* Two and two make four.
- **Permanent Situations**
  - *Example:* I live in London.
- **Habits & Routines**
  - *Example:* She plays tennis every Tuesday.
  - *Time Expressions (Use with):* twice a month, on Fridays, often, sometimes.
- **Short Actions Now** (Commentaries, demonstrations)
  - *Example:* He takes the ball and scores a goal!
- **Future Timetables & Schedules**
  - *Example:* Our train leaves at 11am.
  - *Time Expressions (Use with):* this evening, at 10 o'clock, tomorrow.
- **Future in Time Clauses**
  - *Example:* I won't go out until it stops raining.
  - *Time Expressions (Use with):* until, when, as soon as, after, before.

---

## 2. PRESENT CONTINUOUS

### Usages & Rules
- **Temporary Situations**
  - *Example:* I'm living in London.
  - *Time Expressions (Use with):* at the moment, for a few weeks, for a couple of months.
- **Temporary Habits**
  - *Example:* He's eating a lot these days.
  - *Time Expressions (Use with):* at the moment, these days.
- **Unfinished Actions Now (In Progress)**
  - *Example:* I'm working at the moment.
  - *Time Expressions (Use with):* now, at the moment.
- **Definite Future Plans**
  - *Example:* I'm meeting my father tomorrow.
  - *Time Expressions (Use with):* tomorrow, later, at 7pm tonight, on Tuesday.
- **Annoying Habits / Complaints**
  - *Example:* You're forever losing your keys!
  - *Time Expressions (Use with):* always, forever, constantly.

---

## 3. PRESENT PERFECT SIMPLE

### Usages & Rules
- **How Long (Unfinished Actions / States up to now)**
  - *Example:* I've known Karen since 1994. / She's lived in London for three years.
  - *Time Expressions (Use with):* since 2010, since July, for ten years, for three days.
- **Life Experience**
  - *Example:* I've been to Tokyo.
  - *Time Expressions (Use with):* ever, never.
- **Present Result (Past action with relevant present result)**
  - *Example:* I've lost my keys (so I can't get into my house).
- **Unfinished Time Word**
  - *Example:* I haven't seen her this month.
  - *Time Expressions (Use with):* this week, today, this month, this year.
- **News / Recent Events**
  - *Example:* The Queen has given a speech.
  - *Time Expressions (Use with):* just, yet, already, recently.

---

## 4. PRESENT PERFECT CONTINUOUS

### Usages & Rules
- **How Long (Unfinished continuous actions up to now)**
  - *Example:* I've been living in London for two years. / She's been working here since 2004.
  - *Time Expressions (Use with):* since 2010, since July, for ten years, for three days.
- **Temporary Situations**
  - *Example:* I've been going to the gym a lot recently.
  - *Time Expressions (Use with):* recently.
- **Present Result of a Recent Continuous Activity**
  - *Example:* I've been working (that's why I'm tired).

---

## 5. PAST SIMPLE

### Usages & Rules
- **Finished Actions with a Finished Time Word**
  - *Example:* I went to the cinema yesterday.
  - *Time Expressions (Use with):* yesterday, last night, in 2003, 6 months ago.
- **Details of News**
  - *Example:* I've hurt my leg. I fell off a ladder.
  - *Time Expressions (Use with):* the present perfect.
- **Finished Past Facts & General Past Events**
  - *Example:* Leonardo painted the Mona Lisa.
- **Actions in Stories (Sequence of events)**
  - *Example:* He sat down and ordered a coffee.
  - *Time Expressions (Use with):* the past continuous.
- **Unreal / Imaginary Things (Present/Future Hypotheticals)**
  - *Example:* If I won the lottery, I would buy a house.
  - *Time Expressions (Use with):* wish, it's time, second conditional.

---

## 6. PAST CONTINUOUS

### Usages & Rules
- **Overlapping Action in the Past**
  - *Example:* I was walking to the station when I met John.
  - *Time Expressions (Use with):* when + past simple, two o'clock, 7pm.
- **Story Background**
  - *Example:* The birds were singing and the sun was shining. Amy sat down.
  - *Time Expressions (Use with):* the past simple.
- **Some Past Habits / Annoying Habits in the Past**
  - *Example:* She was constantly singing.
  - *Time Expressions (Use with):* always, forever, constantly, at that time, in those days.
- **Emphasis on Length of Action in the Past**
  - *Example:* I was working in the garden all day.
  - *Time Expressions (Use with):* all day, all evening, for hours.

---

## 7. PAST PERFECT SIMPLE

### Usages & Rules
- **Action Finished Before Another Past Action**
  - *Example:* When we arrived, the film had started.
  - *Time Expressions (Use with):* when + past simple, June, last Tuesday.
- **How Long to a Point in the Past**
  - *Example:* When he graduated, he had been in London for six years.
  - *Time Expressions (Use with):* when + past simple, for + time.
- **Unreal Things in the Past (Regrets & Hypotheticals)**
  - *Example:* I wish I hadn't gone to bed so late!
  - *Time Expressions (Use with):* third conditionals, wish.

---

## 8. PAST PERFECT CONTINUOUS

### Usages & Rules
- **How Long to a Point in the Past (Continuous action)**
  - *Example:* She had been working at that company for a year when she met James.
  - *Time Expressions (Use with):* when + past simple, for + time.
- **Result at a Time in the Past**
  - *Example:* The pavement was wet. It had been raining.
  - *Time Expressions (Use with):* the past simple.

---

## 9. FUTURE SIMPLE (WILL / BE GOING TO)

### Usages & Rules - WILL
- **Promises / Requests / Refusals / Offers / Spontaneous Decisions**
  - *Example:* I'll help you with your homework.
- **Future Conditionals**
  - *Example:* If it doesn't rain, we'll go to the park.
  - *Time Expressions (Use with):* the first conditional.
- **Future Prediction (Opinion / Belief)**
  - *Example:* The sun will rise at 7 am. / I think the Conservatives will win the next election.

### Usages & Rules - BE GOING TO
- **Plans and Intentions**
  - *Example:* A: We've run out of milk. B: I know. I'm going to buy some.
- **Future Prediction (Based on Present Evidence)**
  - *Example:* The sky is getting darker and darker. It's going to rain.

---

## 10. FUTURE CONTINUOUS

### Usages & Rules
- **Overlapping Action in the Future**
  - *Example:* I'll be waiting when you arrive.
  - *Time Expressions (Use with):* when + present simple, at 8pm.
- **Future as a Matter of Course / Routine Future Event**
  - *Example:* The government will be making a statement later.
  - *Time Expressions (Use with):* later, tonight, next week on the 2nd of September.

---

## 11. FUTURE PERFECT SIMPLE

### Usages & Rules
- **Action Finished Before a Point in the Future**
  - *Example:* By ten o'clock, I will have finished my homework.
  - *Time Expressions (Use with):* by three o'clock, by Friday, when + present simple, by the time + present simple.
- **How Long to a Point in the Future**
  - *Example:* When we get married, I'll have known Robert for four years.
  - *Time Expressions (Use with):* when + present simple, by the time + present simple, for + time.

---

## 12. FUTURE PERFECT CONTINUOUS

### Usages & Rules
- **How Long to a Point in the Future (Continuous focus)**
  - *Example:* In April, she will have been teaching for twelve years.
  - *Time Expressions (Use with):* when + present simple, by the time + present simple, for + time.
- **Action Finished Before a Point in the Future with Result**
  - *Example:* When I see you, I'll have been studying, so I'll be tired.
  - *Time Expressions (Use with):* when + present simple, by the time + present simple.

## SUMMARY COMPARISON TABLE FOR NOTEBOOKLM

| Tense | Main Focus / Usage | Key Example | Typical Time Words |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Present Simple** | Routines, facts, timetables | *She plays tennis every Tuesday.* | usually, always, every week, on Mondays |
| **Present Continuous** | Actions happening now, temporary situations, plans | *I'm working at the moment.* | now, currently, at the moment, tomorrow |
| **Present Perfect Simple** | Past action with present result or unfinished time | *I've known Karen since 1994.* | since, for, already, yet, just, ever |
| **Present Perfect Continuous** | Duration of continuous actions up to now | *I've been living in London for two years.* | for, since, recently, lately |
| **Past Simple** | Completed actions at a specific time in the past | *I went to the cinema yesterday.* | yesterday, ago, in 2010, last week |
| **Past Continuous** | Action in progress at a past moment / background | *I was walking when I met John.* | when, while, at 8pm yesterday |
| **Past Perfect Simple** | Completed action before another past event | *When we arrived, the film had started.* | before, after, by the time, when |
| **Past Perfect Continuous** | Duration up to a past moment | *She had been working for a year when...* | for, since, before |
| **Future Simple (Will / Going to)** | Predictions, promises, intentions, schedules | *I'll help you.* / *It's going to rain.* | tomorrow, next year, soon |
| **Future Continuous** | Action in progress at a future moment | *I'll be waiting when you arrive.* | at 8pm tomorrow, this time next week |
| **Future Perfect Simple** | Completed action before a future point | *By 10 o'clock, I will have finished.* | by tomorrow, by 2030, by the time |
| **Future Perfect Continuous** | Duration up to a future point | *In April, she will have been teaching for 12 years.* | by next month for X years |
