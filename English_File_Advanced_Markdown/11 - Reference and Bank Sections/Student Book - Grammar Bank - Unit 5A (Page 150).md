Another way of distancing ourselves from the facts, especially in formal written English, is to use the passive form of verbs like say, think, etc. to introduce them. We can use: It + passive verb + that + clause. Verbs commonly used in this pattern are: agree, announce, believe, claim, expect, hope, say, suggest, and think. subject + passive verb + to + infinitive. Verbs commonly used in this pattern are: believe, consider, expect, report, say, think, and understand. There can also be used + passive verb + to + infinitive.
