Well, if you have symptoms, you go to your GP and they listen to your history, examine you, request investigations, and reach a decision. This process is known as 'diagnosis'. There are few screening tests where 1 the advantages outweigh the disadvantages, and they could lead you to have potentially harmful investigations, or indeed treatment, that you may not have needed.
